blurb: This one has a story, so buckle in!<br><br>You may remember 2020 for one or two reasons. For me, 2020 was the year I had [floroquinolone tendonitis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11858458/) and could barely walk for about 6 months.<br><br>In May of 2020, my then-girlfriend (a floozy) was graduating from University and I was determined to attend. After flying, I quarantined in a friend's empty apartment for a few days. During this time I had absolutely nothing to do but fully take in a wonderful view of the Ohio river from their balcony. Not only that, but this was my first time seeing any nature or wildlife or really anything other than white walls for many months.<br><br>Birds where everywhere, and given I had nothing else to do, I started taking photos. It was my first time trying to take bird photos.<br><br>These photos are not stellar. My kit and form both needed major improvement. But, this was a transcendent experience for me and sent me on the path to today!
