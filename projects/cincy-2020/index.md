blurb: This one requires elaboration, so buckle in!<br>You may remember 2020 for one or two reasons. For me, 2020 was the year I had [floroquinolone tendonitis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11858458/) and could barely walk for about 6 months.<br>In May of 2020, my then-girlfriend (a floozy) was graduating University and I was determined to attend. After flying, I quarantined in a friend's empty apartment for a few days, during which time I was forced to do nothing to do but sit and bask in a view of the Ohio river from their balcony.<br>While this went on, I spotted many birds, and started taking photos. Keep in mind - this was my first time seeing any nature or wildllife or anything other than white walls for many months.<br>These photos are not stellar. My kit and form both needed major improvement. But, this was a transcendent experience for me - and one that set me on a path of bird photography for a long time to come.
