Hello! I am Michael Santacroce. I love taking photos - mostly of birds, but otherwise of buildings, landscapes, or moody urban scenes.

I've taken hundreds of thousands of photos, however, I don't use social media and therefore don't have a good way to share them. Instead, I've:
- Started sending postcards out with some of my favorite bird-focused photos
- Created this website to house images that have already been in postcards or that won't be in a future card (possibly non-avian)

Postcards will be kept for the new hotness and spiciest bird candids. 

However, images too similar to previous postcards can find their way here!

Website will forever be under construction and was originally forked from [here](https://sunbliss.github.io/photorama/).

Thanks!
