Hello! I am Michael Santacroce. If you're here you're either a friend/loved one, or, I've gotten much more popular than expected!

In my free time, I love taking photos - mostly of birds, but otherwise of buildings, landscapes, or moody urban scenes, especially on film.

I've taken hundreds of thousands of photos, however, I don't use (or want to use) social media and therefore don't have a good way to share them. 

Instead, I've started 2 efforts:
1. Postcards will be sent out with some of my favorites, hopefully in themed issues
2. This website will house images that have already been in postcards or that won't make it in a future card (too similar to others; not quite good enough)

Postcards will be kept for the new hotness, so they won't be spoiled by images here. There's a long backlog to get through!

In the distant future, my ultimate goal might be to produce a photo 'zine. 

Website will forever be under construction and was originally forked from [here](https://sunbliss.github.io/photorama/).

Thanks!
